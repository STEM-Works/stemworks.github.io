About STEM Works

STEM Works is an education initiative focused on inspiring students through real engineering challenges. Our programs combine mechanical design, electronics, and programming in a fun, competitive environment.

Our 6-Week Robotics Program

Week 1–2

Intro to engineering, electronics, and CAD fundamentals.

Week 3–4

Hands on robot building, wiring, and ESP32 programming.

Week 5

Testing, debugging, and strategy development.

Week 6

Final competition day.

During Weeks 1-2, students are introduced to our Game Manual, which explains what their robots should be able to accomplish. We provide a demonstration on how to solder wires, followed by a hands-on session where students solder their own connections.

Students also get an introduction to the electrical components used in our program, including ESP32 microcontrollers, LN motor drivers, and servo motors. Additionally, we cover CAD fundamentals using OnShape and guide students in creating 3D models of their robots.

×

During Weeks 3-4, students receive the 3D printed models of the robots they designed in the previous week. They are given time to assemble and manufacture their robots and test whether all the components work properly.

This hands-on phase helps students understand how their CAD designs translate into real robots, and allows them to troubleshoot any assembly issues before moving on to programming and fine tuning.

×

Week 5 is focused on programming the robots. Students start with a test code and must iterate it to match their individual wiring and robot configurations, since each robot is customized.

During this week, we also hold driver tryouts, allowing students to compete in a fun and engaging way while determining who is best suited to drive their robot during the final competition.

×

Week 6 activities: Final competition day, evaluation of performance, awards, and celebration of learning achievements.
